☆ Commissions ☆

Why should you order me something?

If you order an illustration or a painting, you will have a unique piece in your hands that would last forever. You will be part of the project from the very beginning, as you will make aesthetic decisions with me so that we can achieve the result we have imagined together.

I'm also open to work in big projects like books or other kind of artistic projects. If you are interested and need more information about this, contact me.

The communication will be through email.

If you want to make an order, I will need to know:

  • Technique: digital or traditional (gouache, oil, watercolor, acrylic...)
  • Theme: person/character (portrait, bust, full body), group, pet, landscape, cover...
  • Optional additions: background (complex, simple, or none), text, decorations, frames around the image...
  • Style: cartoon or realistic.
  • Dimensions: digital (undetermined, but necessary to know in order to avoid loss of quality if it is to be printed) or traditional (paper: A5, A4, A3, if you want it larger or smaller, you can request it; canvas: undefined).
  • References: the client will provide images of the theme, style, poses... that they like to help achieve the final product they have in mind. A simple sketch from the client helps a lot.

The previously defined parameters will be taken into account, in addition to creativity, time, documentation, materials, etc. and then, you will get the quotation and the time that it will take me to produce it. Once the quote has been accepted and the first payment made, I will begin the work.

Steps

  • After a few days later, you'll get some sketches with the ideas. I accept changes in this part.
  • Final sketch. You decide which one you like the most. This is the last opportunity to make any changes in the drawing; if you want to do something after this, it will be an extra cost.
  • Color palette. I will be doing two or more tests to see what colors will suit best and then, send them for you to choose.
  • Order fulfillment.

Payment

  • A 30% deposit is requested before I start the commission.
  • The rest of it will be required before the delivery.
  • If any of the payments aren't complete, I won't continue or deliver the project.
  • I accept Bizum.

Delivery

  • Digital: you will receive an email with your order in JPG and in PDF. I can print it, but it will be an extra cost.
  • Traditional: you will receive the original piece and a digital copy in JPG and PDF.
  • Physical delivery: the shipping price depends on how far you are. Madrid will be cheaper.
